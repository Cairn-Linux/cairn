# Child test 2: what Phase 0 did not see (P1-17)

A draft plan and a blank notes sheet, written on 2026-11-11 and not yet
run.
A line that starts "Proposal:" is a suggestion, not a decision.
Where each line comes from is at the end.

## What this test is for

Test 1 did not see a child use the Terminal, leave an app with no help, or
meet the "Something needs a grown-up." screen.
This test watches for those, for Steam games with Steam's overlay off, and
for the rest of "What to look for".
No image leaves the family before this test and two other checks are done.

## Before you start

- [ ] The build on the laptop: ______________________________________
- [ ] Each child has a letter, never a name:
  Child A (age __, level __), Child B (age __, level __).
- [ ] No photos, recordings or screenshots of a child.
- [ ] Each child's Steam is signed in, with its overlay off.
  See "Getting Steam ready".
- [ ] The brightness is set from your own login.
  A child's keys cannot change it.
- [ ] No grown-up is logged in.
  Log out of your own login; do not use Switch User.
- [ ] Each child has been shown Super+Q and Log out once.
  When: ______________
- [ ] On each child's sheet: online or offline, and charger or battery.
- [ ] How long each child's turn is: ______ minutes
- [ ] A clock, a pen, and this page.

## During

You take notes.
You do not step in unless a child asks.

- Write the time, what happened, and what the child said in their own
  words.
- When a child asks for help, help.
  Write what they asked and what you did.
- When "Something needs a grown-up." comes up, wait.
  Watch what the child does first.
- Do not point, name a key, read the screen aloud or take the mouse.
- Do not type your computer password on a child's screen.
  Cairn never asks for it there.
- Proposal: step in unasked only when the screen shows something a child
  should not see, or when a child is upset. Write that you did.

If an app is stuck, **Ctrl-Alt-Home** ends it and brings the tiles back.
Holding the **power button** for five seconds turns the laptop off.

## After

- Ask each child: what did you like, what was confusing, and what would
  you want it to do?
- Ask the younger child whether each game's picture tells them which
  game it is, and whether they want tile names read aloud.
- Write up what happened the same evening.
- A finding becomes an issue only when the maintainer says so.

### While a child uses the computer

#### 1. A child opens something from the Terminal

- **Wait for:** a child presses the Terminal tile and types.
  The tile is left as it is for this test.
- **Counts as seen:** the child types a command such as `open draw`, and
  the app or game opens. No grown-up typed, spelled or pointed.
- **Write down:** every word typed, as it was typed, and what a child who
  cannot read yet does with the tile.
- Seen / not seen: __________  Notes: ______________________________

#### 2. A child leaves an app on their own

- **Wait for:** a child wants to stop an app or a game.
- **Counts as seen:** the child gets back to the tiles with no help, by
  Super+Q or by the app's own way out.
- **Write down:** which way, and each time an app closes, whether the
  child meant to leave. Test 1's notes could not say.
- Seen / not seen: __________  Notes: ______________________________

#### 3. A child meets the grown-up screen and presses Back

- **Wait for:** the screen that says "Something needs a grown-up.", names
  what did not start, and has a Back button.
- **Counts as seen:** the child gets back to the tiles with no help.
  Back, Escape and Super+Q all do it.
- **Write down:** what the child did first, what they said, whether they
  fetched you, and any other line on the screen.
  Count on the child's sheet how many times the screen came up.
- Seen / not seen: __________  Notes: ______________________________

#### 4. Steam games with the overlay off

- **Wait for:** a child plays a Steam game for a few minutes.
- **Counts as seen:** no box from Steam comes up over the game, and
  nothing of Steam's friends, chat or community shows at any time.
- **Write down:** any box that does come up, and what it says.
  In one test with the overlay on, Steam's notice came up in the corner
  of the game about 15 seconds after the tap.
- **Grown-up's part, outside a child's turn:** press Shift+Tab in a game
  and write what happens. With the overlay off it should do nothing.
- Seen / not seen: __________  Notes: ______________________________

#### 5. A grown-up fixes Steam from their own login

- **Wait for:** the grown-up screen with the extra line "Log out, then a
  grown-up can fix it from their own login."
- **Then:** follow "When Steam turns a sign-in down", under "Steps for
  the grown-up".
- **Counts as seen:** the child logged out, you signed their Steam in
  from your own login, the child logged in again, and the game started.
- **Write down:** how long it took, and whether the steps worked as
  written.
- Seen / not seen: __________  Notes: ______________________________

#### 6. A game's picture tells the younger child which game it is

- **Wait for:** the younger child chooses a game.
  Afterwards, at the tiles, ask which game is which.
- **Counts as seen:** the child opens the game they meant, and names or
  points to a game from its picture.
- **Write down:** any game opened by mistake, any tile with a name and no
  picture, and whether a child who reads looks for the name.
- Seen / not seen: __________  Notes: ______________________________

#### 7. Whether the pictures pull the children away from Draw

- **Wait for:** nothing. Watch what each child opens.
- **Counts as seen:** each child's sheet has the first tile opened, and
  the minutes in Draw and in games.
- In test 1 the game tiles had no pictures.
  Both children opened Draw first on the first evening, and the younger
  child went straight to a game on the second.

#### 8. A child at the tiles with the battery low

- **Wait for:** the laptop off the charger, a child at the tiles, and the
  sentence "The battery is low. Ask a grown-up for the charger."
  It is due at 20%.
- **Counts as seen:** a child is at the tiles while the sentence shows.
- **Write down:** whether the child noticed it, what they said, and
  whether they asked for the charger.
- Nothing shows over a running game, so watch the battery yourself.

#### 9. Whether a child who cannot read notices the battery's fill

- **Counts as seen:** the notes say whether the child looked at it or
  spoke of it unasked.
- Proposal: afterwards, at the tiles, ask what the drawing at the top is,
  and what the plug means when it shows.

#### 10. A sibling at the login screen and the grown-up's tile

## Steps for the grown-up

### Getting Steam ready

Do this before the day, for each child, from your own login.
The child must be logged out.
`docs/TRY.md` says more about **Steam for**; step 3 is from #74.

1. Open **Steam for** and the child's name from your application menu,
   and press **Open**. It asks for your password.
2. If the child's Steam is not signed in, sign in with the child's Steam
   account and tick **Remember me**.
3. Turn the overlay off: Settings, then In Game, then the switch "Enable
   the Steam Overlay while in-game".
   Cairn does not turn it off yet (#74).
4. Press **Done**. The window's own close button can lose a game's last
   cloud save.
5. Log out. Do not use Switch User: it leaves your session open behind
   the child's.
6. Proposal: log in as the child once, start one Steam game from the
   child's own tiles, and press Shift+Tab in it.
   With the overlay off it should do nothing.
   If a sign-in made this way does not reach the child's tiles, this test
   runs with no Steam (ADR-0049).

### When Steam turns a sign-in down

These are the steps in `docs/TRY.md` for the line "Log out, then a
grown-up can fix it from their own login."
`docs/TRY.md` says they have not been run as written yet.

1. The child presses **Back**, then **Log out** above the tiles, and
   **Log out** again when it asks "Log out now?".
2. At the login screen, tap your own tile and type your password.
3. Open **Steam for** and the child's name from your menu, and press
   **Open**. It asks for your password again.
4. Sign in with the child's Steam account and tick **Remember me**.
   If Steam asks for a Steam Guard code, type it.
5. Press **Done**, then log out. Do not use Switch User.
6. The child taps their tile at the login screen, then the game.

### What the computer wrote down

From your own login, this shows what the launcher and Steam wrote today.
Match its times to the times on the sheets.

```sh
journalctl -t cairn-launcher -t cairn-kiosk -t cairn-steam-for -t cairn-steam-visit --since today
```

## Already decided

The maintainer decided these on 2026-11-02, so they are not asked again
below.

- Steam games are in this test (ADR-0049).
- When Steam turns a child's sign-in down, the child logs out and a
  grown-up fixes it from their own login (ADR-0049; #88).
- Steam's overlay is turned off by hand in each child's Steam (#88; #74).
- The Terminal tile stays as it is.
  The test watches what a child who cannot read yet does with it, and the
  choice in #83 is made after the test (#83).

## Not decided yet

Only the maintainer can answer these. None has an answer on this page.

1. **Which children, and at which levels?**
2. **Is a level 2 account tried?**
   Level 2 looks the same as level 1 for now (`docs/TRY.md`).
3. **Which build is on the laptop for the test?**
   The checklist has the blank.
4. **Is the laptop online for the whole test?**
   On test 1's offline evening, twelve of thirteen Steam presses started
   nothing.
5. **Does the test wait for #25, #74 or #102?**
   #8 asked for #25 and #74 to be complete before a child test with
   Steam, and for #102 to give a check that can be run again.
   All three were open on 2026-11-12.
6. **How long is each child's turn?**
   Test 1's rule was twenty minutes (ROADMAP P0-9).
   On its second evening the younger child stayed about 45 minutes.
7. **If no child opens the Terminal unasked, is one asked to try it?**
   Proposal: ask once, near the end of the turn, and write that you
   asked.
8. **Is the grown-up screen waited for, or arranged?**
   Proposal: wait first. `docs/TRY.md` names one way it comes: offline, a
   Steam game with cloud saves does not start, and that game stays held
   until the child logs out and back in.
   Entry 5's line comes only when Steam has turned a sign-in down.
9. **Is the laptop off the charger for part of the test?**
   Entries 8 and 9 need the battery in view, and entry 8 needs it low.
   `docs/TRY.md` says to keep a laptop plugged in, because nothing warns
   during a game.
10. **Is a sibling asked to press the grown-up's tile?**
    #8's row says to have a sibling use the login screen and the Guardian
    tile.
    Proposal: leave the login screen up and wait, and ask only if nobody
    goes near it.
11. **Does entry 14 still apply?**
    A game from the family's Steam folder gets a tile that starts it
    through Steam (`provision/README.md`), and #8's row asks for a game
    in ScummVM itself.
12. **When are the grown-up's checks run, and on which computer?**
    Proposal: on the laptop, after the children's turns or on another
    day, never during a turn.
13. **Are the steps for entries 15, 17 and 18 written down before the
    test?**
    Freezing a game, making a stray window and cutting off a level change
    have no written steps for the laptop.
14. **One evening, or more?**
    P0-9 asked for the test to be repeated at least once, and test 1 took
    two evenings.
15. **What makes this test passed?**
    ADR-0050 waits until child test 2 has "passed". The P1-17 row lists
    what to see and sets no bar.
16. **Is there a laptop with 4 GB for entry 12?**
    It is not required (ADR-0031).
17. **Does entry 13 stay?**
    No doc says this test must see it.
